# Scenario 03 — Geospatial Fire Intelligence

## Product context
A geospatial intelligence application for exploring wildfire risk, hotspot evidence, environmental layers, and time trends around areas of interest.

## Primary journeys
1. Select an area of interest.
2. Explore risk and environmental layers.
3. Inspect hotspot or area evidence.
4. Compare observations over time.
5. Export or share an evidence-based summary.

## Required screens
- Map-centric analysis workspace
- Layer/filter controls
- Feature/evidence inspector
- Temporal analysis
- Report/export surface

## Platform targets
- Web desktop
- Tablet
- Phone for field/glance use

## Design challenge
Avoid placing a map inside a generic dashboard card. The map is the primary work surface. Evidence freshness, data provenance, and uncertainty must be visible and understandable.

## Acceptance criteria
- Map remains the dominant spatial reasoning surface.
- Layer controls are discoverable and do not obscure critical geography.
- Selected-feature evidence is easy to inspect.
- Time and source provenance are explicit.
- Phone layout adapts to field use with focused panels/sheets.
- Map interactions have keyboard or text-based alternatives.

## Visual differentiation prompts
Compare Geospatial Cartographic, Data Intelligence, and Organic/Eco-Tech profiles. Do not equate environmental subject matter with automatically using green.
